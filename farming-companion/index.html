Sources & calculations

Routes compare estimated travel times, not measured world records. Unlocks, shortcuts, loading, run energy and house layout can change the result.

Herb XP includes harvest XP, the final-harvest planting equivalent and 36 XP for ultracompost. Herbs have no health check or gardener payment. Your yield estimate includes any equipment bonuses; they are not applied a second time. Use the tool leprechaun to note herbs.

Patch and transport coverage informed by Herb Run Planner.

Crop XP includes a health check and replanting. Selecting harvest adds six fruit, or your estimated Celastrus bark. Unprotected trees are assumed to survive. A filled bottomless bucket uses half an ultracompost item per application.

Rune pouch teleports require the standard spellbook, the spell’s Magic level and any quest unlocks. Law, lava and mist runes cover the elemental requirements of the standard teleports used here. Catherby uses a separate tablet.

Personal records are grouped by included patches and the travel and equipment used. Crop types do not create new groups. They are personal comparisons, not proof of equal in-game conditions.
