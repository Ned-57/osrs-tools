RuneRoute

Paste your OSRS Wiki DPS loadout, enter the TTK the Wiki gives you, and RuneRoute turns it into an inventory plan, trip estimate, profit forecast and upgrade shortlist.

1. Import your Wiki setup

In the OSRS Wiki DPS calculator choose Share → Copy link, then paste it here.
Paste a link and import it. Your gear will appear here.

2. Tell us the result

RuneRoute doesn't try to reproduce the Wiki combat engine. Enter its average TTK and we handle the trip maths.

3. Upgrade lens

Suggestions are generated from the imported gear. Price is fetched live when the Wiki price API is available. TTK changes are estimates until you verify the swap in the Wiki DPS calculator.
The Wiki calculator has boss-specific accuracy, integer max-hit breakpoints and special-case mechanics. RuneRoute identifies sensible swaps automatically, but the Wiki remains authoritative for the exact new TTK.
Profit assumptions
Gross loot/kill is editable because drop-table EV moves with GE prices. Rune costs and prayer potion prices are fetched live where possible.
